Why Subject-To Works for Florida Sellers

Many Florida homeowners locked in low interest rates (3-4%) during 2020-2022. If you need to sell but don't want to lose that rate, a subject-to deal lets you transfer the property while keeping the existing mortgage in place. The buyer takes over payments, you walk away clean, and you often get more money than a traditional cash offer.
